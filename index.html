Our Story

Two decades of crispy tradition

Sakshi Enterprises was born in a small kitchen with a big dream — to bring the authentic taste of Indian namkeen to every household. Today, we craft our beloved snacks using the same time-honoured recipes, locally sourced spices, and the same love that started it all.

From the morning crunch of fresh Gathiya to the evening delight of our Sakshi Mixture, every product carries the warmth of home.
